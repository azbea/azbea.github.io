## Design changes and rationale

- Kept the obsidian and ember palette, cinematic atmosphere, starfield, glass surfaces, grain, and scroll motion. The relaxed lock allowed clearer mobile layouts and link treatments.
- Added circular arrows and descriptive secondary labels to link rows/cards; corrected → internal and ↗ external semantics; added visible focus rings, pressed feedback, one-time entry nudges, and reduced-motion behavior.
- Added a local inline SVG sprite for GitHub, LinkedIn, Telegram, and Instagram and used the marks in contact rows, the drawer, and footer.
- Made the language switcher preserve the current route and localized the menu’s dynamic accessibility labels. Added mobile spacing/type adjustments for Uzbek and Cyrillic labels.
- Added 41 marked draft passages per language (123 total), including three unpublished journal drafts in each language. `?samples=1` outlines draft copy. Journal and Gallery remain `noindex`.
- Added Unicode-range declarations for Latin, Latin Extended, U+02BB, and Cyrillic. Only Inter is preloaded. FontTools is not installed and HarfBuzz could not load the WOFF2 files, so actual glyph coverage remains unverified.
