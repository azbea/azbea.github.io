( Biography · 01—10 )
01

Where it began

Azizbek Abbosov was born on 19 June 2007 in Uzbekistan. His early education began in his home country.

02

Early education

He attended Secondary School No. 2 in Chust from 2014 to 2023. His school education was completed in 2023.

03

Learning and academic development

From 2023 to 2025, Abbosov studied at Chust District Polytechnic No. 2. He graduated with honors and received a red diploma.

04

Aviation and study at KAU

Since September 2025, Abbosov has studied Aviation Management (Pilot Program) at Korea Aerospace University. He completed semesters one and two of the first academic year.

05

A period in South Korea

Abbosov spent approximately three years in South Korea. His studies at Korea Aerospace University were part of that period.

06

New academic and professional directions

His education also includes ongoing Banking studies at Tashkent State University of Economics. The program is online, with some examinations held offline.

07

Exploring technology

Abbosov independently learns and builds across frontend, mobile and backend development. His skills include HTML, CSS, SCSS, JavaScript, React, Vue.js, Next.js, React Native, Node.js, Express.js, NestJS, Python, FastAPI, REST APIs, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Git, GitHub, Docker and Figma.

08

Teaching and communication

He worked as an English language instructor at an education center in Tashkent. His IELTS Academic score is 7.0.

09

International ambitions

Aviation and technology are among Abbosov’s interests. Alongside formal study, he independently learns and builds practical digital projects.

10

The road ahead

Abbosov continues Banking studies at TSUE while developing independent digital projects. Aviation, technology and useful software remain central interests.
